# Meridian

See what's still missing. Close part of it.

Meridian is an Arizona classroom remaining-need ledger. It shows the exact physical items still needed, lets a community close part of a line through verified shipping or in-person handoff, and places relevant Arizona education policy beside the classroom.

**This is Code Arizona, not Hack Arizona.**

The product thesis, users, journeys, and differentiation live in [`docs/product.md`](docs/product.md).

The public count is **item-level verified fulfillment**, not a fundraising percentage.

| Seeded Weather & Climate Lab line | After reset |
| --- | --- |
| Dry-erase markers | 8 / 20 verified |
| Construction paper packs | 10 / 10 closed |
| Student thermometers | 4 / 20 |
| Poster paper rolls | 1 / 5 |
| Student lab notebooks | 12 / 30 |

Submitting 5 markers does **not** make the ledger 13 / 20. The teacher must verify first.
